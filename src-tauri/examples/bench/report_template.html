Yank Search Benchmark

How search works now

The old pipeline asked Jev to re-rank a keyword (BM25) shortlist. When you describe a clip in different words than it contains, BM25 never puts it on the shortlist, so no re-ranker can find it. The new pipeline finds candidates by meaning first, on your machine, and only then asks a judge model to pick.

Parse

Date and type words

"yesterday" becomes a date filter, "links" a soft category boost. Unchanged.

Stage 1 · on device

EmbeddingGemma

Every clip is embedded once (4-bit, ~200 MB). A search scores all clips by cosine, plus a small keyword nudge.

Stage 2 · optional

Jev or Laya picks

The top matches go out as one Choice question. Jev (cloud) decides the order; Laya (local MLX) is blended 50/50 with stage 1.

Fallback

Never blocks

Model still downloading, or re-ranker down: you get stage-1 or keyword results instead of an error.

Why the old pipeline couldn't get there

A re-ranker can only promote what the first stage hands it. For paraphrased queries, BM25's top 50 held the right clip in few cases; EmbeddingGemma's top 50 almost always holds it.

Choosing the on-device model

Fourteen local embedding models on the same corpus (full-corpus cosine, no re-ranker). EmbeddingGemma wins on accuracy and recall at a query cost of ~16 ms; its 4-bit build loses almost nothing against full precision at a sixth of the size.

Choosing the re-rank question

Same shortlist, different ways of asking. One Choice over the whole shortlist matched per-item questions on accuracy with a third of the tokens. Local cross-encoders trained on web search barely helped. Laya alone is a weak judge of clipboard relevance. Blended 50/50 with the embedding score it helps, fully offline: +3.8 points in this exploration, +1.6 in the final app pipeline, where the stronger document text had already closed part of the gap.
